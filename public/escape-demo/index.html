ABOUT ESCAPE

A studio built on stillness, strength, and community.

Studio atmosphere
photo placeholder

Escape Yoga Studio was founded by Annerita van Zyl with a simple intention: to create a space where people could step out of the noise of daily life and reconnect with themselves.

Located in The Willows, Pretoria, we offer a full weekly schedule of classes, seasonal workshops, and immersive retreats designed to meet you exactly where you are.

"Yoga is not about touching your toes. It is about what you learn on the way down."
